## Principles

1. "The amount of complexity I'm willing to tolerate is proportional to the size of the problem being solved." — [@ry](https://tinyclouds.org/rant/)
2. "There should be one-- and preferably only one --obvious way to do it." — [@tim-one](https://mail.python.org/pipermail/python-list/1999-June/001951.html)
3. "Programmer time is expensive; conserve it in preference to machine time." — [@esr](https://www.catb.org/esr/writings/taoup/html/ch01s06.html)

## Timestamp

Use ISO 8601 ordering with dash-separated fields for display and filenames,
with either day precision (`YYYY-MM-DD`) or second precision (`YYYY-MM-DD-HH-mm-ss`).
